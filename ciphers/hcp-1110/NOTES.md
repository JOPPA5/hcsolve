## 2026-11-03 — first examination (Claude Opus 5.5, solver)

Files: work/hires/*_adr.jpg (message side, 3468x2298) and *_pic.jpg (address side; the portal's
"pic" image is the printed Königreich Bayern 5-Pfennig Postkarte, not a picture). Crops in work/crops/.

Address side:
- Printed postal stationery; the 5 Pf stamp is imprinted, so there is no stamp box to lift.
  Postmark ILLERTISSEN ... 86; arrival STUTTGART on the message side.
- Along the left edge, in ink, Kurrent, written vertically: "Illertissen 17 Dezem[ber] 1886" and
  "Ingenieur K....e" (the surname is not secure: K r/n e(with u-bow?) y/z l/e; perhaps "Kneyle" /
  "Kreuzle" — not resolved). This is plain, unenciphered writing (date line and title/name).
- On the dotted address lines there is only pencil pseudo-script: loops imitating m/n/l
  ("mm  mm  ull / in mm ull / m"). No letter shapes can be separated; there is no address that
  could be read.

Message side:
- Seven long pencil lines plus four short groups and a "signature" made only of zigzag strokes
  (m/n-like waves). No distinct letters, no repeated symbol inventory, no digits: this is
  pseudo-writing, not a substitution alphabet. Top right a small "2" (page/card number?).
- Under/between the heavy zigzags there are VERY faint grey traces of earlier finer handwriting
  (loops of ascenders visible e.g. line 2 near x≈600–1500 px of the hi-res). Tried: grey autocontrast,
  blue channel, background subtraction (Gaussian 25/40), percentile clipping, smoothing + 1.5–2x
  enlargement. The traces show only isolated loops and stems; not a single letter, let alone a word,
  can be read. Whether this is erased writing (then over-scribbled to hide it) or offset from another
  sheet cannot be decided from the scan.

Conclusion: no ciphertext with a sign inventory exists on this card. The visible "message" is
deliberate pseudo-writing (a hoax / concealment), and any real message under it is not recoverable
from the available scans. Possible approach: raking-light or IR imaging of the original (private
collection, T. Schrödel). Status: partial; no marker, no register entry.
The Jolánka "under the stamp" steganography note does not apply: the stamp here is printed on the card.
