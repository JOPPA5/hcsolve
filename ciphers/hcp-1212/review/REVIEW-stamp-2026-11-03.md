## Is there writing under the stamp?

Yes. In `footprint_green.png` the GYÖNGYÖS postmark breaks off sharply after "GYÖ". Its upper-right arc is missing. The circle comes back only along the bottom, at the decorative band. This gives a blank rectangle that reaches the top edge of the card, and the red-ink note sits inside it. The cancel struck the stamp, the stamp was later removed, and the paper underneath carries the note but no postmark ink. So the note was hidden under the stamp. The type change to "MASC + Steganography" is justified.
