**English:** Túrkeve, 7 May 1913. Beloved dear Jolánka! I received your card, which I accepted with thanks, and you write that you had a good time. I am very sorry that I cannot go [unread word]. I cannot write anything else. My respects to your dear parents. I remain, respectfully, Andor.

**Slovak:** Túrkeve, 7. mája 1913. Milovaná drahá Jolánka! Váš lístok som dostal, prijal som ho s vďakou, a píšete, že ste sa dobre zabavili. Veľmi ma mrzí, že nemôžem ísť [neprečítané slovo]. Viac napísať nemôžem. Pozdravujem s úctou Vašich drahých rodičov. Zostávam s úctou Andor.

## Uncertain

1. Line 10, the end of `mehetek…ital`: *mehet* is clear, then a looped t, a round vowel (e or o), a k whose stem is crossed by the descender of *hogy* above it, a small hooked sign (r or c), then *ital*. The word break and meaning are unsettled (*ki tál*? *ki által*?); I do not force it. Perhaps a place or person.
2. `oz nagyom`: first word is o + descending z, taken as *az*(t). The m/n hand makes *nagyon* and *nagyom* look the same, and *nagyon* is what the sense needs.
3. The looped t (looks like a b with a crossbar, as in `Szeretett`, `Tisztelettel`, `Mást`) and the k-like z after a tall s (`Szeretett`, `Szép`) are this writer's letter forms, not cipher signs: both appear inside ordinary words, and a sound-for-sound reading gives Hungarian throughout.
4. `Szép` on the address is read from a tall s + z + e + p; `urlánynak` = *úrlánynak* ("to Miss").
5. Whether a message is hidden in some way I cannot see (stamp, invisible ink) cannot be excluded from a scan. The stamp is partly torn away and shows no writing. The picture side, contrast-equalised, shows only a printed number (3601/02 or similar), the publisher's mark, a fingerprint and the collection stamp.

## Why no cipher is claimed

Every line reads directly as Hungarian with no key. None of the Jolánka family's signs (two bars = a, three bars = e, and so on, hcp-1216) occur here, and the text is a polite reply by "Andor", a sender not otherwise known in the family's solutions I checked. Applying the family key is meaningless since the signs are not there.
