## English of the secure parts

Beloved sweet Jolánka! I wish that [my lines] find you in the best of health. I received your esteemed lines, for which I thank you very much. I am truly sorry to keep you waiting... I have no time or opportunity for writing... I already answered all your lines in my earlier card... My dear, you have again dressed carelessly... the greatest trouble. In vain I ask you to take care; from now on take better care of yourself. As for my tooth, it no longer hurts, thank God. Next time it hurts I will try the doctor... Do write to me.

## Uncertainties

Everything in brackets. Word sense decided h vs p ("hiába", "hogy") and the "tehetek róla" reading runs under cancel lines. Word division, accents and repairs are editorial. The gaps are whole clauses: postmark rows 5 and 7-12, stain rows 25-31, tail rows, stamp rows, right-edge signs. The pencil note at top centre (starts "Sz") is unread.

## Why this much is believed

The family key gives connected, period Hungarian with no adjustment across long independent passages, and the address and "Feri" agree with the family. That supports the key, not the gaps.
