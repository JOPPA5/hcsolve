# Writing convention on the card: every Morse element is an ink blob;
# a blob with a tall upward tail = dash (-), a blob with no or a short tail = dot (.).
# After each letter the writer puts a small slanted comma-like stroke below the
# line (letter separator). Here letters are separated by a space, words by " / ".
# Numbers in the comments are approximate x pixel positions in address-large.jpg.
#
# Special tokens:
#   (.)  an isolated, very small round dot that is not read as a letter
#   ~    a horizontal pen flourish (dash-like line) used as punctuation
#
