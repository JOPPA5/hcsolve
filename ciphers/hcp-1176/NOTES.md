# Notes hcp-1176

2026-11-01: captured by Claude Opus 5.5 (orchestrator).

2026-11-01 (Claude Opus 5.5, solver): full inspection of both hires scans. RESULT: NO CIPHERTEXT FOUND ON THIS CARD.
- Address side: everything is plain Hungarian (greeting, dated 1915/IX/29, a numbered moral poem, closing, address). Brown horizontal scribbles run over every line, including the address; they cross out, they do not encode. The dark letters ("kollak", "soha", "sorsod") are where that wet overstrike met the blue ink, so I did not read them as marks.
- Unlike the sister cards (hcp-1174, 1175, 1178, 1179) there is no line of digit groups. Those cards also show brown overstrike and the same "Kedves Margitkám/Ángyika" address; hcp-1175 has the same "Sz. A." signature. So the family's digit cipher is simply absent here.
- Stamp area: stamp lost, paper bare; only one short ink stroke, no writing (work/crops/stamp.png, contrast-stretched).
- Picture side: quadrant sweep with equalised contrast (work/crops/p1-p4.png) found no handwriting. The green curls on the skirt (work/crops/skirt*.png, curl_raw.png) are the same green broken line as the fold highlights and the blouse trim, in all rotations open scrolls, not signs; I take them as printed/applied dress ornament. This is a judgement from the scan, not proved; they can't be matched to digits or letters in any orientation I tried.
- Red "14?73" lower left of the address side is a printed publisher number, not text by the writer.
- Consequence: no key can be recovered here, and this card gives no crib for the digit cipher except that the writer's style and correspondents are the same. No marker created. Plain transcription is in SOLUTION.md.
