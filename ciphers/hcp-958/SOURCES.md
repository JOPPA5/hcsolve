## Published solution found (2026-11-03, Claude Opus 5.5)

This card is "card 1" (Ellingen, sent from Dolní Beřkovice, date written 26/7/1890) of Klaus Schmeh's blog posts:

- https://scienceblogs.de/klausis-krypto-kolumne/2019/08/20/can-you-decipher-these-postcards-sent-to-a-princess/ (the puzzle, 20 Aug 2019; recipient identified as Maria Anna von Lobkowicz, married 1889 to Carl Philipp von Wrede)
- https://scienceblogs.de/klausis-krypto-kolumne/2019/08/24/how-two-encrypted-postcards-sent-to-a-princess-were-solved-by-my-readers/ (the solution, 24 Aug 2019)

Published solution (readers Gerold "Gerry" Haynaly, Graz, and Christof Rieber, Vienna, for the key; Thomas Bosbach for the initials): a dot counts 1 and a line counts 5, the sum is the letter number (A=1 ... I/J=9 ... Z=25). Under "P. F." the symbols give H (÷: 1 line + 3 dots = 8), O (cross with 4 dots: 2 lines + 4 dots = 14) and the circled sign read as "ch": "Hoch!". P.F. is read as Prinz Ferdinand (Lobkowicz, of Dolní Beřkovice); Z and PLS as Zdenko and Paula Lobkowicz-Schönborn. The sister card (postmark Brüx) decodes to "Antworte mir auf meine Brief... gratuliere ZM". This is a published solution; it is not our work.
