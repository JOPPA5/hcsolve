# hcp-958 — SKIPPED: published solution exists (not our work)

Status: not solved by this repo. Per README step 3 the card has an already published solution, recorded here and in SOURCES.md; no DONE marker is created.

Published in Klaus Schmeh's blog (24 Aug 2019, readers G. Haynaly, C. Rieber, T. Bosbach), see SOURCES.md for URLs.

- Cipher: dot/line number code (dot = 1, line = 5, sum = letter number, A=1, I=J=9).
- Picture side: "P. F." / "÷ | ⁜ | ⊖ || !" / "Z. L. - B. L. S. - T. L. - Z. L. - E. L. Ch. L." / "18 26/7 90".
- Published reading: "Hoch!" (H = 5+1+1+1, O = 5+5+1+1+1+1, circled sign read as "ch"); P.F. = Prinz Ferdinand Lobkowicz; the bottom line is initials of Lobkowicz family members (Z = Zdenko, P.L.S. = Paula Lobkowicz-Schönborn, per Bosbach; the others not individually identified there).
- My pixel check (hi-res scan, work/crop_sym.png): ÷ has one line and three dots, the cross has two lines and four dots, so 8 = H and 14 = O agree with the published key. The circled-minus sign (circle + one line) does not follow the dot/line arithmetic; "ch" is the publishers' word-sense reading. The initial I read as "B" in "B.L.S." is what Bosbach reads as "P"; the scan shows a looped capital that could be either.
- Address side: Fürstin Wrede-Lobkowitz, Ellingen, Bayern; plain, no hidden text seen in the printed stamp box (the 2 kr stamp is printed, nothing can be under it).
