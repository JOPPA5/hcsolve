Slovak: Milá Ángyika! Mnohokrát Vás pozdravujem a najšťastnejšie veľkonočné sviatky želám drahej (k.) ángyike aj malej Mariske; ešte raz zo srdca pozdravujúc všetkých, s mnohými bozkami zostávam milujúci brat Vášho švagra, Árpád. Mezőkövesd, 22. apríl 1916.

Residuals: "k." expanded as kedves is my guess (kis/kedves). Signature "Árpád" is the least sure word: A, r, p, a, d are legible through the stroke, but the accent on A is not seen and a mark above the signature is taken as the á accent. "sógor fivére" is read as two words; written close, sense fits either way. Surname reads Szegedy on the card (portal: Szegedi). 1916-04-22 was Holy Saturday (Easter 23 April), which fits the greeting. "húsvéti" is written without a clear accent on u (huswéti-like shape is a normal v), as on the printed picture side "Husvéti".

Sister cards: the montage of hcp-1174, 1175, 1176, 1178, 1179 shows the same scribble over clear text; several of them (1174, 1175, 1178, 1179) also carry digit-group lines, which are the real cipher of this correspondence. 1175 opens with the same "Kedves Ángyika!". This card has no such lines. The scribble seems to be a habit of the writer rather than a key. Whoever works on the digit cards may use this card only as a hand and vocabulary sample.
