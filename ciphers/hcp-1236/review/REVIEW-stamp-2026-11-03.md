## Physical state (steganography)

- At the top right there is a pale rectangle with perforation scallops at its corners (most visible at the top left and bottom left). This is the footprint of a stamp that has been removed.
- The ring of the right-hand postmark (left arc, with "...GA...") and its lower fragment stop at the edge of the rectangle and do not continue inside it. So the stamp was on the card when it was cancelled.
- The five lines of pink ink lie entirely inside the rectangle. The stamp therefore covered them, and they were hidden when the card was sent.

So there is writing under the stamp, and the type change to **MASC + Steganography** is justified. The register entry already carries that type.

## Language

"Csókolja ... számtalanszor Feri" is idiomatic Hungarian for a postcard of that period ("Feri kisses [you] countless times"). *távolba* is literally "into the distance". The solver's gloss "from afar" (normally *távolból*) is a reasonable reading of colloquial usage, and the solver labels it as colloquial. The literal form is reported faithfully. The translations are acceptable.
