### English (read part)

Dear Jozsi! We got home safely, only I caught a bad cold and I am ill. God be with you, until we meet again, I kiss you, your sister Ilonka. Address: To the Honourable Jozsef Toth, watchmaker's journeyman, Ujpest, corner of Jozsef and Istvan streets.

### Slovak (read part)

Mily Jozsi! Stastne sme dorazili domov, len som sa velmi prechladila a som chora. S Bohom, dovidenia, bozkavam ta, tvoja sestra Ilonka. Adresa: Ctenemu panovi Jozsefovi Tothovi, hodinarskemu tovarisovi, Ujpest, roh ulic Jozsefa a Istvana.

## Not read (letter shapes as seen; these are not readings)

1. Line 9, under the signature: "lapot meg kap tatejat" by shape. "lapot" (card, postcard) and "megkap-" look likely; the ending is not understood.
2. Line 10: "De nem ... semmi(t) ...oltol/altal" by shape, roughly "De nem ug..semmit fri..ltal". Middle and last words not established.
3. Top line: "fel egynefelyes" by shape. Possibly "fel" or "fel egy" plus something like "nefelejcs" (forget-me-not), which is only a guess.
4. Right edge, two rotated lines: "fetorhatan" or similar, then "taka" or "tala". Not understood.

## Other uncertainties

- "es beteg vagyok" rests on word sense: this hand writes a and o alike, r and n alike and v close to n.
- "novered" (your sister) reads clearly, so Ilonka writes as a sister, though the surname is not on the card.
- The picture side had only an overview check; a faint mark there is not excluded.
