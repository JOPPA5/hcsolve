## Cipher and key

Monoalphabetic substitution, with one special family glyph for CH. Shape labels are
conventional: `m` stands for N and `n` for M. `H` labels the special CH glyph (ř-like
shape), not an ordinary H. `F` labels the shallow-v glyph, which decodes to F.

| Shape | Plain | Shape | Plain | Shape | Plain | Shape | Plain |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Y | 2 | U | 3 | O | 4 | I |
| 5 | E | 6 | A | 7 | J | 8 | L |
| b | P | c | S | d | T | f | V |
| g | K | h | R | m | N | n | M |
| p | B | q | Z | r | H | s | C |
| t | D | H (special) | CH | F (shallow v) | F | | |

Glyph notes: `s` (C) is the short teardrop with no tall ascender. `h` (R) has a tall
looped ascender. `b` (P) is the b-shape and `p` (B) the descender p-shape. `q` (Z) is
written like "9".

## Reproduction

From the repository root (Python standard library only, read-only):

```sh
python3 ciphers/hcp-1534/work/decode.py
```

The command checks the SHA-256 of the originals, the manual record
(`work/transcription.json`, `work/key.json`, `work/indexed-units.csv`) and two
reference files. It decodes every row, checks each indexed position and runs a
tokenwise inverse-key round trip, then prints the literal text. Current output:
**24 rows; 564 indexed marks; 564 assigned units; 0 unresolved; 3 CH units; 567 base
letters; round-trip OK.**

The transcription is manual, not OCR. The record is generated by
`work/build-record.py`; round-1 row labels are archived in
`work/r2/build-record.round1.py`. Row boxes refer to `source/picture.jpg` rotated 90°
counterclockwise (674×1024). The round-2 check crops (`work/r2/row_*.png`, row sheets
`work/r2/sheet_*.png` and word zooms `work/r2/z_*.png`) come from
`work/r2/rowcrops.py` and `work/r2/zoom.py`.

## Uncertainties

- **U02 MNE (after TVOJE).** The first sign is an n (M) written over an earlier sign.
  The blob has an n-arch on the right and a descender about 7 px below the baseline.
  An n has no descender. The writer probably began a sign with a descender (perhaps p
  = B, an early start of "blaží") and overwrote it with n. The word has three
  positions, the same as the clean `nm5` earlier in U02, so it is read and counted as
  MNE (`work/r2/z_u02mne.png` against `z_u02nm5.png`). No letter is inserted for the
  abandoned sign.
- **S01 VASKERI.** The second sign has a short top flag. It is close to the 5 in the
  same word, and the 6/5 distinction is uncertain at this resolution
  (`work/r2/z_s01.png`). It is read as 6 (A), as in round 1. Reading 5 would give
  VESKERI directly. The editorial word is *veškeří* either way.
- **L01 MILAVANE.** The fourth sign is a clear б (6 = A), where MILOVANE would need 3.
  It is kept literally as a writer's slip and read editorially as *milované*.
- **Other corrections in the writer's hand** (each read as the final sign):
  S10 AVSAK, third sign a c written over an f-like stroke (`z_s10.png`); L03 SLIBENOU,
  first sign c heavily blotted (`z_l03slib.png`); U04 OBKLOPUJICIM, overinked g, and
  the 7 written over another stroke (`z_u04.png`).
- S04 DOUFAL uses the shallow-v F glyph. It was first inferred from context and then
  matched to the published F of card 199 (see provenance).
- Accents and punctuation are editorial. Diacritic marks are not separate cipher
  units. The nature of the "výroční schůze cecilská" and of the "koncert své práce" is
  not established by the card. A church-music (Cecilian) society meeting is
  plausible, but nothing on the card confirms it.

## Why the reading is believed correct

The family key reads all 564 units into continuous, grammatical Czech of c. 1903 with
period vocabulary (srdéčko, holubička, údové, schůze cecilská). The register and
phrasing match the other Pepouš / Anna Tichá cards, including the closing formula
"věčně jeho Pepouš". The only literal deviations are two single-vowel slips and one
overwritten sign, all disclosed above.
