# HCP 980 — PARTIAL (no decipherment)

Status: partial. No plaintext is claimed. No DONE marker.

Card: Theux (Belgium) -> Arlon, postmarked 10 Aug 1907, to Armand Boutet,
14e de ligne 3/3, Arlon. Portal status Not solved; no transcription or
solution attached; no published solution found (short web search).

What is established:
- The message is the 5-line block of dot/dash/stroke glyphs left of the
  address; provisional glyph transcription in work/transcription.txt
  (manual, from the hi-res scan; L5 partly smudged and illegible).
- It is not plain International Morse letter-by-letter, not a Caesar-shifted
  Morse, and not the dot-count Pigpen family used on hcp-1131/1189/1190.
- A simple substitution on the glyph shapes is the most likely type; cribs
  such as MON + LOULOU/TOUTOU (line 1) and UNE (line 4) agree with one another
  but do not extend to a consistent key for lines 3 and 4.

See NOTES.md (2026-11-03) for every hypothesis tried and why it failed.
