## Editorial Czech

Accents, capitals and word joins are editorial. *srdečko* and *Hradce* contain the restored C.

Rozmilá má Aničko, vřelý dík za drahý lístek. Přála jsi si dnes již odpověď, avšak já teprve dnes jsem lístek tvůj dostal. Byl teprve včera odeslán z Hradce a došel včera večer do Prahy, kdy se již nedoručovalo. Rmoutí mne, že moje srdečko opět stůně. Přivodila jsi sobě chorobu tu sodovou vodou, které já nevěřím. Šetři se co nejvíce, drahoušku. Tisíc vřelých, vášnivých pus posílá ti stále vzpomínající Pepouš. Slečně Jankové vyřiď, prosím, moje poručení.

## English translation

My dearest Aničko, warm thanks for your dear card. You wished to have an answer already today, but I only received your card today. It was only sent off yesterday from Hradec and arrived in Prague yesterday evening, when there were no more deliveries. It grieves me that my little heart is ill again. You brought that illness on yourself with soda water, which I do not trust. Spare yourself as much as you can, darling. A thousand warm, passionate kisses are sent to you by your ever-remembering Pepouš. Please give Miss Janková my regards.

## Uncertainties

1. **R04 HRAD[C]E.** After `rh6` (HRA) a crossed looped `t` (D) is read. The next position is a blot one sign wide, then `5` (E). The C is restored from context only: *z Hradce*, "from Hradec", the recipient's town (Jindřichův Hradec). No stroke of that sign is recovered.
2. **R06 SRDE[C]KO.** Between `5` (E) and `g3` (KO) stands one heavy sign: a closed round bowl crossed by a long oblique stroke, like a struck or overwritten sign. Its shape does **not** match the family `s` (C) on this card. The C is restored from context (*srdečko*, "little heart"), which fits one sign position. Whether the stroke was meant as a háček is speculative; it is not used as evidence.
3. **Manuscript gaps** in NEDORUCO VALO, PRI VODILA and VASNI VYCH are rejoined in the editorial text.
4. **Marks over CH/PUS** in R07/R09 are recorded but not interpreted. All accents in the editorial text follow standard Czech.
5. **STUNE / stůně** is the period form of *stoná* ("is ill"). **PORUCENI / poručení** is the period word for "regards, compliments" (literally "commendation").
6. The sender is known only as Pepouš (Josef). Janková is the K. Janková named on the address side.

## Why it is correct

The fixed family key decodes all 361 read signs into continuous, idiomatic Czech of 1903 with no unmapped sign. The text agrees with the card: the recipient in Jindřichův Hradec, the Prague postmark, the host Janková named in the address, and the family signature Pepouš.
