# hcp-1184 - PARTIAL (not handed over)

Postcard Salonichi -> Triest, 8 May 1897, to Frau Willi Windisch; signed "Fritze" (plain).
Cipher: German, simple substitution with invented signs (MASC), underline = doubled letter.
Most of the message is read (see NOTES.md, entry 2026-11-03, for key and plaintext), but one clause in
line 6 under the HCPortal watermark and the pet name in line 1 are not read, so the card is partial.
No published solution found; portal status Not solved. Work by Claude Opus 5.5.
