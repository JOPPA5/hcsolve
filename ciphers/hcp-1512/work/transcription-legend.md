# Manual glyph normalization, corrected after review 1

This is a manual reading of source/address-large.jpg, not attached text or
OCR. The reviewer's independent reading was checked against the scan and
review details and adopted for the corrected transcription. The first line
is the separate header; the following twelve are the body. Spaces mark
editorial word divisions. INSIDE and WHILEI have no clear internal gap in
the source; WHILE I is a harmless reading-space interpretation.

N is the eta/n-shaped N-code; Y is the fork-shaped S-code. Lowercase c
identifies the open curled C-code, Q the closed-bowl/descender R-code.
Several C/R instances are close at source resolution and rely partly on
context. R decodes E, E decodes Z. B-shape decodes W; I-stroke decodes B.
The first glyph in BRcPZYR really is B-shaped: retain WECAUSE.

8 is the two-loop P-code, seen in 8WY0TNS = POSTING. It is distinct from
B (W). % explicitly records the Phi-shaped retraced/overwritten A in EARLY;
it maps to A as the P-shaped normal A-code does. There are 24 observed visual
IDs but 23 base glyph types/plaintext letters. The final R (E) of INSIDE has
a vertical overwrite; it remains R in the transcript, with this alteration
explicitly recorded here. The earlier pen strokes cannot be reconstructed.

The apostrophe in DAY'S, stops after HOLIDAY and DOINGS, and comma after
THURSDAY are preserved. Punctuation is not counted among 281 cipher positions.
