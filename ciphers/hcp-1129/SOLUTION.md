# HCP 1129 — SKIPPED: published solution exists

Status: not solved here. Per README step 3 this card is skipped because a published solution of this exact card was found.

Published solution (not ours): SEDLMAIER NICHT GESEHEN ("[I have] not seen Sedlmaier"), proposed by reader "Gerry" in the
comments of Klaus Schmeh's blog post of 11 Nov 2019
(https://scienceblogs.de/klausis-krypto-kolumne/2019/11/11/an-encrypted-postcard-with-only-21-letters/), endorsed by other
readers; see SOURCES.md. Mechanism as described there: invented glyphs built from a loop, a stem and added dashes, each added
dash advancing one letter of the alphabet (bare loop = A).

Light consistency check only (Claude Opus 5.5, 2026-11-03, on work/hires pic scan): the card has 9 + 5 + 7 signs plus a final
dot; sign 1.6 is a bare loop (A in the published reading); signs 1.2, 1.8 and 3.6 share one form (loop + stem + one top dash),
matching E in SEDLMAIER / GESEHEN. Sign 3.4 is partly covered by the HCPortal collection stamp. No independent decipherment,
no key table, no command and no marker were produced.
