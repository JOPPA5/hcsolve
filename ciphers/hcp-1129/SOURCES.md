## Published solution found (2026-11-03, Claude Opus 5.5)

- Klaus Schmeh, "An encrypted postcard with only 21 letters", Klausis Krypto Kolumne (scienceblogs.de), 11 Nov 2019:
  https://scienceblogs.de/klausis-krypto-kolumne/2019/11/11/an-encrypted-postcard-with-only-21-letters/
  Same card (Ingolstadt 1899 to Fräulein Therese Ernst, Unterhausen; 21 signs on the picture side; scans from T. Schrödel).
  In the reader comments, "Gerry" proposed the plaintext SEDLMAIER NICHT GESEHEN (a stroke-built alphabet where each added
  dash advances one letter; the bare "o" is A). Norbert, Christof Rieber and Thomas endorsed it; Thomas noted a firm
  "Ernst & Sedlmaier" (lime works) in Unterhausen near Neuburg an der Donau. Read via WebFetch summary on 2026-11-03.
