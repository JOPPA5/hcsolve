The card (Paris, 12 April 1921, to D. Hill, Silverdale, Keymer, Hassocks, Sussex) carries an ordinary plain-English message:

"How would you like to go for a ride in this coach. I saw it the other day. Hope you are enjoying your holiday. Give my love to Ken. yours Sally"
