# Glyphs are written in VISUAL left-to-right order, exactly as on the card.
# "." = the dots the writer scatters between letters (nulls; dots above/below the line not recorded).
# "|" = a visible word gap.  Plain lower-case letter = an ordinary cursive letter.
# Bracketed tokens = word-initial flourished forms (all stand at the RIGHT end of their word,
# i.e. they were written first in right-to-left writing):
#   [T:x]  T-sign, form 1: long crossbar with two short legs (pi-like capital T); always t/T
#          (think, this, Tuesday)
#   [B:x]  form 2: long crossbar with a single 3-/z-shaped stem descending below the line;
#          x = value read from context: b (bit, be) or h (hope).  Split from [T] after review 1.
#   [Y:x]  looped sign with long tail (y in "you", q in "quite")
#   [X]    crossed loop sign standing alone = the word "a"
#   [A]    loop with descender = initial a of "are"
#   [W]    w-forms (in "well", "with")
#   [N]    3-shaped sign = initial n of "night"
#   [L]    horizontal loop = initial l of "love"
#   [S]    open loop = initial S of "Sha"
#   [F]    looped ascender without crossbar = initial f of "fright"
#   [D]    capital D
#   [U]    "M"-like final u of "you"
