# HCP 1539 / pc_hcp_220 — Czech postcard from Pepouš to Anna Tichá, 10 Sep 1903

**Status (2026-10-30, after review 1): complete reading.** All 13 cipher rows
(406 signs) decode with the Anna Tichá family key. One residual uncertainty: the
second sign of the pianist's given name, L{O|E}LA (LOLA preferred).

This is a manually transcribed Czech picture postcard, dated 10. IX. 1903,
addressed to Anna Tichá, teacher temporarily in Pelhřimov, with the instruction
"Vlastnoručně dodati" (to be delivered into her own hands). The sender signs
Pepouš, as on the other cards of the family. Portal metadata and acquisition are
in SOURCES.md.

**Credit.** The key is the family key known before this card was transcribed:
transferred from earlier family cards in this repository
(ciphers/hcp-1520/work/key.json) and the published partial Anna Tichá study by
Maxim Egorov (https://maxim-egorov.dev/research/anna-ticha-postcards/). It is not
an independent key discovery. No published solution of this card is known; the
portal attaches none and lists it as Not solved. Initial transcription and
decipherment: OpenAI GPT solver (GPT-6 Astra / GPT-6.1 Sol), 2026-10-28;
completion (2026-10-29) and the corrections after review 1 (2026-10-30): Claude
Opus 5.5 as solver.

shapes. `H` is the ř-shaped sign for the digraph CH; `y` is the 7/J-shaped sign.

## English translation

> Hearty thanks for today's card, which crossed with mine. Have both my cards
> of the 2nd and 3rd of this month really been lost? I have been asked to take
> part, playing the violin, next month in Vamberk, where an academy (concert)
> is to be held in memory of the late bandmaster Vyskočil; Mrs Maturová, the
> pianist L[o]la Beranová and cousin Vyskočil are also to perform there. But I
> could not promise anything definite, and most likely I shall not take part.
> Pepouš sends you a thousand kisses. Please give Miss Janková my regards.

   a closed loop, read 3/O (LOLA, preferred) with 5/E (LELA) possible. Recorded
   as the single bounded alternative `{3|5}`.
2. **Writer's spellings kept in the literal text:** `c3` = SO for *se* (row 2);
   NESUCASTNIM for *nezúčastním* (row 11, s for z).
3. Accents are editorial. The persons (Vyskočil, Maturová, Beranová, Janková)
   and the Vamberk academy were not identified from historical sources. The
   sender is known only as Pepouš.
4. The key was transferred, not recovered blind from this card; it decodes every
   sign without change and no sign is unmapped.
