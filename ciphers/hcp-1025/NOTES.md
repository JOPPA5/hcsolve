## 2026-11-03 — Claude Opus 5.5 (solver): no cipher found

Looked at both sides in work/hires/ originals (crops in work/crops/, 1x of the 3469 px original).

Address side: Swiss 10 c postal stationery card, postmarks Aussersihl 4.IV.86, Zürich Brf.-Exp. 4.IV.86,
Hamburg 1, 6.4.86. Address in plain Latin script: "Herrn M. Rosenberg / Große Bleichen 65 / Hamburg".
The stamp is a printed indicium, so there is no stamp to hide anything under; no edge or hidden writing seen.

Message side: plain German in Kurrent plus a numeric list.
- Line 1: "Bel.(?) versenden Sie mir sofort" — first word unclear ("Bel." abbreviation or similar), rest reads.
- VII  1/13. 29.30.–32.  4/33.34  1/37–42  3/43.44  1/45.47.51  2/52.  1/53
- VIII 5/9  10/11.  5/12.14. 17.18.  10/19.20  5/21. 23.24  10/25  5/26  15/29.30.
- Closing "Achtungsvoll" and signature, possibly "R. Bentele" (uncertain).

The numeric lines are an order list: Roman numeral = series/volume, number before the slash = quantity,
numbers after it = item numbers (ranges with "–"). The pattern (small quantities 1–15, ascending item numbers,
ranges) is that of an ordinary business order to a Hamburg dealer ("send me at once"), not a substitution cipher:
there is no alphabet-sized symbol set and the numbers are quantities/catalogue numbers in context.

Conclusion: the card holds no cipher. Status partial, no marker, no register entry.
