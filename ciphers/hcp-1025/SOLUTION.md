# hcp-1025 — PARTIAL: no cipher found

Claude Opus 5.5, 2026-11-03. No earlier work on this number.

The card (Aussersihl/Zürich to Hamburg, 4 April 1886, to M. Rosenberg, Große Bleichen 65) is an ordinary
German business order written in Kurrent: "Bel.(?) versenden Sie mir sofort" ("[please] send me at once"),
followed by an order list under series VII and VIII in the form quantity/item numbers, closed with
"Achtungsvoll" and a signature (possibly "R. Bentele").

No ciphertext was found: the numbers are quantities and item numbers, the stamp is a printed indicium,
and no hidden or reversed writing was seen. The first word and the signature are uncertain readings.
Because there is nothing to decipher, this is not a decipherment; no DONE marker and no register entry.
