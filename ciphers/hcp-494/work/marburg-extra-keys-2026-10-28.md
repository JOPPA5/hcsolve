## Concrete comparisons

- **1238 scan3** includes Bonaparte among proper names numbered4000+, consistent with its nineteenth-century catalogue date. **Scan5** has A119, E296, B184, unlike255.
- **1220 scan5** has A36/63/90/99, **Dannemark252**, **Holstein424**. **Scan16** instead gives **Dannemark904**, **Holstein153**, while numbered slots601,634,768 are blank. These are incompatible word lists under the unchanged target values.
- **1218 scan141** and **269** each contain an isolated20=A, but the surrounding alphabet differs. This is not a match to255.
- **1218 scan375** has A16/40/64, B20/44/68, C24/48/72: here20 means b. **Scan392** has A39/63/87 and BB=Engelland, so doubled capitals alone do not imply the target letter alphabet.
- **1218 scan286:**601=Consentez.
- **1218 scan355:**601=Durlach Stadt, between600=Durlach Markgrafschaft and602=Markgraf zu Durlach. The working reading of634 is einnehmen.
- **1218 scan399:**768=fünf/fünff, followed by fifteen, fifty, five hundred and five thousand.
- **1218 scan422:**601=Danck/Dank (working reading), whereas **Dennemarck621**, dänemarkisch622 and König in Dänemark623. This is a different numbering, not confirmation of target601.
- **1218 scan477:**601=Beratschlagen and634=Besoldung.

The target601/634/768 meanings remain fallible annotation-derived screening hints. The primary selection criterion was the unchanged255 alphabet. No selected alphabet matched it. The listed mismatches establish differences for particular tables, not an exhaustive exclusion of each archival file.
