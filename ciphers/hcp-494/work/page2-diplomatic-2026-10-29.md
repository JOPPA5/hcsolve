## What the original wording constrains

There is **one cipher code on this page**, provisionally630. The other visible numerals24,3 and4 belong to a prior-letter date and travel/waiting intervals. The brown text treats630 as a masculine personal referent: `der ... gegenwärtig`, followed by `selbiger`, `seine reception` and `Er ohne abschied`. This supports a person in a reception/departure dispute; **it does not independently recover the nameBlumenthal**.

Root reports that original page0003 begins `davon gangen wäre`; combined with the re-inspected0002 ending, the working continuation is **`Er ohne abschied / davon gangen wäre`**. Only0002 was inspected by this helper. See [ending detail](page2-diplomatic-2026-10-29/ending-detail.png).

The clear body also records arrival fromFlensburg, audiences and civil treatment, but departure `sine rerum gerendarum nervo`, promises of a modest `wechßelgen(?)`, and an intention to wait at most four more days before going toBerlin. These are contextual constraints, not new numerical key values.
