# Danish cipher corpus: images 76–150

28 October 2026. Solver triage, not an independent review or full transcription.

All 75 images were inspected on nine numbered contact sheets. Selected images
77, 83, 97, 98, 114, 127, 137, 143 and 149 were additionally opened individually;
83, 97 and 98 were rotated by 90 degrees with an exact pixel transpose.
The contact sheets are downscaled overviews; individual readings below use the
separate image views. No matching HCP 494 key was identified in this bounded pass.

Concrete checks against adoption:

- Image 77: A=5, B=13; Danemarc=31, La France=103 and La Suede=117.
- Image 83: A=1320, B=1330; Dennemark=3 and king=5. The low numbers are words,
  unlike the HCP comparison key's low nulls. No values are transferred.
- Images 97–98: A=299/106, B=289/116; the dense syllable/word list is different.
  The displayed 634 entry on image 98 reads `son`, not the auxiliary Holstein
  reading of HCP 494. This is a manual working reading, not a full transcription.
- Image 114: A=26, B=21, with graphic alternatives; Denmark=45 and Sweden=137.
- Image 127: A=98/97/96, B=77/78. This is not the target alphabet.
- Image 137: a single graphic substitution alphabet, not the target's numeric
  homophones plus doubled capitals.
- Image 143: A=12/13/14/15, B=10/11, E=1/2/3/4. King of Denmark=212.
- Image 149: A=37/38/29, B=36/39/30; Denmark=6, Holstein=99, Sweden=164.

The different alphabets do not by themselves exclude reuse of a separate word
list; several word entries were therefore checked too. The observed anchor
conflicts are reasons not to adopt those lists, not proof excluding every
mixed-key theory. No Lincker/Hesse endorsement establishing the target family
was identified. Unread small entries, damaged regions and unsampled detailed
word lists remain outside this screening claim.

Inputs, exact URLs and hashes are in
`danish-witness-2026-10-28/download-manifest.json`. Reproduce these overviews with
`danish_root_sheets.py` in a Pillow-enabled Python environment. New downloads
remain in the separate research bundle; the existing 431 source files were not
modified. No plaintext or nomenclator assignment changes follow from this pass.
