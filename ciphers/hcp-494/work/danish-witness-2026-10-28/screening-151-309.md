# Danish key corpus, pages 151–309: bounded visual screening

No HCP 494 key or accepted new plaintext was identified. All **159 assigned viewer pages** were seen in contact sheets, and **21 selected scans** were examined individually. This is layout and sample-value screening, not a full transcription or exhaustive exclusion of the corpus. The companion JSON records every selected page and the scope.
