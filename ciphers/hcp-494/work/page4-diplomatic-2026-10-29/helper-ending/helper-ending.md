the lower quarter, plus the short preceding Lit. B / desiderio clause at the
parent solver's request. This helper did not consult Daniel's transcription,
the current solution, or historical lookup. Black glosses visible in the

`[word?]` denotes a tentative reading; a descriptive bracket denotes an
unresolved span. Capitalization, spacing, and punctuation are provisional.
Uncertain expansions are not silently introduced.

## Brown-ink draft, preserving line breaks approximately

```text
Secretarius Raumer an mich begehrt[?] gibt die beylage
Lit. B. und wird [Anredeabkürzung unaufgelöst] Cantzlar schon wißen wie weit Ihme
hierinnen in seinem desiderio zu fügen. Der [selbige?]
427. 641. war auch zu Hamburg, und hat selbiger
einer estroite[?] 303. zwischen 447. 602. und 834;
unter handlung, und wird selbige ehistes tages zum
stande kommen. Hiermit[?] [Anredeabkürzung unaufgelöst] Cantzlar Göttl:
Obhuth; mich aber dero beharrl: affection empfehle
Meines insonders hochgeehrten Herrn Cantzlar[s?]
```

The strongest contextual reading here is **“schon wißen wie weit Ihme /
hierinnen in seinem desiderio zu fügen”**. It supplies ordinary letter prose;
it does not decipher an additional code. The adjective after **Der** before
427/641 is **not resolved**: `selbige` is only a visual candidate. It must not
be promoted because of a black gloss or the known person identification.

The final negotiation clause is relatively clear: **“unter handlung, und
wird selbige ehistes tages zum / stande kommen.”** It expresses an expectation
that the matter will shortly be concluded, not evidence that the projected
agreement was actually concluded.

The closing has **Göttl: / Obhuth**, **dero beharrl: affection empfehle**, and
**Meines insonders hochgeehrten Herrn Cantzlar[s?]**. The address abbreviation
before Cantzlar is deliberately left unexpanded. Compare `prose2.png`,
`prose3.png`, and `formula-detail.png`.

## Dateline

```text
Hamburg

a° 1672
```

The **4**, **May**, **1672**, and **Hamburg** are legible. A small mark follows
the 4; it has not been securely resolved as a second day number. **This
particular image reading does not independently establish “4/14 May”.**
An ordinal/scribal abbreviation remains possible. This is not a claim that
the larger historical dating is wrong. See `date.png` / `date-detail.png`.
