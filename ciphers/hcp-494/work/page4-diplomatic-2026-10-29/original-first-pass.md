## Reading qualifications

- Lines1–7: word and abbreviation readings require checking. In particular the glyphs after `576 mit` are not silently decoded as a person or treaty. The final digits of829/651 are partly crossed by later ink; these canonical candidates are not wholly new independent readings.
- Line11: the monetary/unit sign and the short word beforeSchilling are unresolved. They should not be normalized merely from a known fiscal formula.
- Lines15–21: token labels are a working transcription convention, not a claim of decipherment. BB/PP, II/55, 8/au and terminalXX/ff questions stay open. The upper part of the looped glyph is partly overlain by black ink; no hidden stroke is reconstructed. Punctuation dots are preserved schematically, not treated as a cryptographic modifier.
- Line17: `nun?` could be another short linking word; do not use it to repair the surrounding syntax. `edell / bürger` crosses a line, so whether it forms an uninterrupted compound or an enumeration also remains unresolved at this stage.
- Line19: the Latin adjective is deliberately left unresolved between the competing visual readings; `olim non erat ita` and `hostis` are substantially clearer.
- Lines23–24: `schon wißen wie weit Ihme / hierinnen in seinem desiderio Zu fügen` is independently supported by the helper. The adjective afterDer is not secure. The original clearly saysAnhaltische and supportsRaumer, but supplies no given name.
- Lines26–27: `unter handlung` is the literal spacing seen; its intended grammatical form is not silently rewritten. The blackKayser annotation is not used to decide how the adjacent447602 codes should expand.
