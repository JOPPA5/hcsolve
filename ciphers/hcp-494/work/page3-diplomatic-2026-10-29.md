## What the brown text actually constrains

The previous page ends `Er ohne abschied`, joined here to `davon gangen wäre`. The narrator claims to have mediated a reception dispute; the unnamed visitor then dined twice at the king’s table and received a present of500 currency units. Code625 helped despite poor prior treatment at the court designated by653. That treatment included a portrait valued at no more than70 and the absence from a recreditiv of the predicate `Edlen` used in the king’s credential. This links625 to a specific earlier diplomatic mission, but **does not name that person**.

The later passage discusses diplomatic ceremony at829 and630’s dispatch, followed by a prospective declaration in favour of774 and775 sending ambassadors. The final word on the next page remains uncertain, so no destination is inserted here. In particular, the hypotheses that625 is Ahlefeldt or Habbæus require independent evidence matching the event, not simply a compatible diplomatic career.
