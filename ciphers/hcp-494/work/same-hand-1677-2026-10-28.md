# HCP 494 and the 1677 Lincker letter: bounded handwriting check

28 October 2026. Solver helper, not independent review. This check uses existing photographs only; no new witness was acquired.

## Result

The 1677 letter **cannot currently be promoted to an authenticated same-hand control** for HCP 494. Shared broad German cursive forms make a relationship possible, but the selected comparisons do not supply distinctive enough agreements to identify the writer. Nor do the differences prove two different writers. Catalogue attribution to Lincker concerns correspondence/authorship; it does not establish who physically wrote either letter.

More decisively for this route, the inspected 1677 encrypted inserts contain **numeric tokens only**, under a different numeric/graphic table. They supply **no independently identified doubled PP, BB, II, XX or special ff sign**. Even a future same-hand attribution would therefore not directly settle the target's ff/XX ambiguity from these inserts alone.
