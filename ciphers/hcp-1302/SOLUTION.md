## Cipher and key

Hungarian, monoalphabetic graphic substitution of the Jolánka family. The key is the one in `ciphers/hcp-1216/SOLUTION.md` and `ciphers/hcp-1301/SOLUTION.md`, transferred unchanged. The plain address side reads Szépremény[ű] Tóth Jolánka úrleánynak, Jász-Árokszállás, in the same pink hand. The end of Szépreményű runs under the cancel (the y is visible, the ű is not). Tóth Jolánka úrleánynak is plainly written; the place is spelt with a flourish after "Jás", and its end runs under the portal seal. Hand and ink fit the family.

Variants on this card: k appears as both the 8-shape (kám) and an S-shape (kor, kis); g appears with a looped head in row 3. Dotted and undotted crossed curves were both met, so i/o rest partly on the marks and on word sense.

## Reading (normalized)

Szeretet[t] édes Jolánkám! Hogy találta magát vasárnap? Én elég jól mulattam, igaz, hogy én hamar feltalálom magam. Itt volt [Á]rokszállásból kis angyalka is, és elég jól mulatott. Fél ötkor mentek el a mulatságból, én is hatkor mentem haza. Csak az fáj, hogy nem Jolánkámmal mulathattam, de ezen nem segíthetünk, már mi nem zárhatjuk ki a cselédeket se a mulatságból. Jöjjön e[l] 20-ikán, ott majd nem lesz kevert né[p][s]ég. Isten vele, a viszontlátásra.

Under the stamp: Csókolom számtalanszor. Sírig [h]ű imádója, Feri.

## English

Beloved sweet Jolánka of mine! How did you enjoy yourself on Sunday? I had quite a good time; it is true that I quickly find my feet. A little angel from Árokszállás (or a person called Kis Angyalka: the Hungarian allows either) was here too, and she had quite a good time. They left the party at half past four, and I too went home at six. Only it hurts that I could not have my fun with my Jolánka, but this cannot be helped, for we cannot shut the servants out of the party either. Do come on the 20th; there will be no mixed crowd there then. Farewell, until we meet again.

Under the stamp: I kiss you countless times. Your admirer, faithful unto the grave, Feri.

## Slovak

Milovaná sladká moja Jolánka! Ako ste sa zabavili v nedeľu? Ja som sa zabavil celkom dobre, pravda, ja sa rýchlo nájdem. Bol tu z Árokszállásu aj malý anjelik (alebo osoba menom Kis Angyalka: maďarčina pripúšťa oboje) a zabával sa celkom dobre. O pol piatej odišli zo zábavy, aj ja som o šiestej išiel domov. Len to ma bolí, že som sa nemohol zabávať so svojou Jolánkou, ale tomu nepomôžeme, veď ani služobníctvo nemôžeme vylúčiť zo zábavy. Príďte dvadsiateho, tam potom nebude zmiešaná spoločnosť. S Bohom, dovidenia.

Pod známkou: Bozkávam Vás nespočetnekrát. Váš do hrobu verný ctiteľ, Feri.

## Uncertainties in the newly read passages

1. **Row 19 *né[p][s]ég* is the weakest word on the card.** The clear signs are n, é, then at the end é, g (a tick stands above the three bars). Between them: (a) a blot with no recoverable shape, taken as a struck false start, no value; (b) a tall stroke with a foot and a shorter stroke to its right, which is the h/p class, read p; (c) a heavy overwritten mass joined to that short stroke, in which no s can be seen. The s is a restoration of a hidden sign, not a reading: *nép?ég* gives a word only with s. The h/p call and the word as a whole rest on sense (*kevert népség*, mixed crowd, which answers the complaint about servants at the party). *Névleg* was considered and gives no sense here. If the reviewer rejects the word, this is the one un-understood word in the sentence.
2. **Row 19 *lesz*:** the sign before z is a small closed-headed bowl, heavier than the open right bowl of row 7 and row 1. It was read as gy in the first attempt, but no y stroke exists on it and the next sign is an ordinary z, so s + z is the reading. Value by shape plus word sense.
3. **Row 18 *e[l]*:** the card has *jöjön e*, with one j and no l. Normalized *Jöjjön el*, the writer dropping letters as elsewhere. *e [hó] 20-ikán*, "the 20th of this month", is the alternative; the sense is the same.
4. **Row 18 *20-ikán*:** the numeral 2 has a crossed foot, then a large 0. Then a dotted crossed curve i, an S-shaped k written over its tail, two bars a whose lower bar grows from the k, a high accent tick, and n. All signs are seen; they overlap. The dot below the n may be a full stop. The form *-ikán* with numerals is in the same family, on hcp-1298.
5. **Row 18 *ot*** normalized *ott*. Whether the writer meant *majd nem* or *majdnem* cannot be told from spacing; *majd nem lesz* is taken from sense.
6. **Stamp row S4 *sírig [h]ű*:** s (blotted but its shape is seen), i, r, i are visible. Then one curved fragment fits the left of g, then the paper is abraded with one faint arc, then a plain cross with a dot, so u with a mark, read ű. The g is partly seen and the h is restored; *hű* is the word it makes with the visible cross, and *sírig hű* is on hcp-1216. Its presence on the sister card suggested the reading, so this is not an independent one.
7. **Stamp row S3 *lan[s]zor*:** the sign after n is a dark blot, read s by word sense. z (only its arms survive around the abrasion), the top of o, and r are visible.
8. **Stamp rows S1 and S2:** the l of *csókolom* is a smear; the third stroke of m in *számta* is abraded.
