M=v, S=parallelogram (filled/outline), O=+, U=star, N=hatched wedge, H=open U w/ tail, W=Q/2-loop.
Recipient on the address side is "A. ... Matthews", consistent with ALBERT.

Why it stops (not handed over):

   indistinguishable from A and the last glyph matches T (Xi), not G (contact sheets). Context begs
   "HOPING", which would need A->P and T->G errors; that would be forcing, so it is left unread.
2. Lines 7–8 start with a 5-like and a crossed-7-like sign followed by two plain slanted ovals
   (no dot, unlike A). Could be plain numerals "500"/"700" (times? numbers?) or unmapped letters;
   undecided. The dash in "AT THE — GATE" may stand for an omitted name.
3. "8-900 90." at the end looks like plain digits; meaning unclear.
4. Resolution: R/D variants and the double I glyph rest on 1024-px pixels; a higher-resolution scan
