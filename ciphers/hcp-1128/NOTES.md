## 2026-11-03 — Claude Opus 5.5 (solver), partial, no marker

- Card: Győr, dated in plain "Győr. 1947. XI. 1-2." on the address side's left edge. No stamp, no address in plain writing, nothing written on the picture side (printed "Glückliches Neujahr" only; edges and picture checked on hires crops).
- Plain note in brown ink/pencil at top centre: "Sz. Imádlak" (Hungarian "I adore you"; "Sz." read as Sz, uncertain).
- Cipher-like marks (full transcription in work/transcription.txt): a black-ink line on the message half, written along the long edge: `/ . U . / T / ' F .`; a blue-ink block on the address half in four rows on the printed dotted lines: `/./././././`, `| U / T / U / T /`, `| i | .i. | .¦. | . | .¦.`, `B. /./././.`. Signs used: long slant/upright strokes, short strokes, dots, T and inverted T, one |- sign.
- Tried and rejected:
  - Sign = letter (MASC): row 1 would be ABABABABA and row 2 ABACABAC; that cannot be Hungarian. The left line pattern (1=5=7, 2=4) does not fit "Szeretlek", "Imádlak", "Sz. imádlak".
  - Jolánka stroke key (hcp-1216 family: two bars a, three bars e, inverted T j...): the classes do not occur here except the inverted T, and the regular alternation stays.
  - Morse (/ and | = dash, . = dot): no visible letter spacing; row 1 = -.-.-.-.- and row 4 = -.-.-. read only as repeated C/K/N/T, no Hungarian.
  - Long slashes as letter separators, contents as letters: row 1 = 4 identical cells, row 2 = alternating ⊥/⊤; no language.
  - "Word shape" reading (ascender/x-height marks along the dotted baseline) cannot be inverted uniquely and is not testable.
  - Hidden writing on the picture side or under a stamp: there is no stamp and nothing on the picture.
- The blue block sits exactly where the address goes, and the 4th row starts with "B." (perhaps Budapest, or the signature initial). The regular patterns look more like a decorative or private dot-and-stroke code with too little text to break than like a substitution of a sentence.
- Web search for a published solution (2026-11-03): nothing found for this card.
- Stop: no reading of any cipher sign. Status partial; no DONE marker, no register entry.
