## Method and key

The omitted-J German Pigpen convention was deliberately transferred from the already read related cards **HCP 995 and HCP 1016**. This is a disclosed key transfer, not an independent rediscovery of that key and not use of a supplied HCP 905 plaintext. The opening immediately gave `IA VERD ... NICHT EINMAL DIR KANN ICH'S MEHR RECHT MACHEN`. The remaining rows were then transcribed and audited against that same key. No per-word remapping was introduced.

The shape names describe the strokes drawn: SE=┘, S=⊔, SW=└, E=⊐, O=□, W=⊏, NE=┐, N=⊓, NW=┌. A period after a shape name means an **interior dot**.

| Shape | SE | S | SW | E | O | W | NE | N | NW |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plain | A | B | C | D | E | F | G | H | I/J |
| Dotted, attested | K | L | M | N | O | P | - | R | S |

The dotted NE cell is not attested on this card and is not needed by the decoder. Additional shapes: upright stroke `I` = T, check-like V = U, left angle `<` = V, upward wedge ∧ = W, dotted upward wedge = Z. Two short strokes above the A or U shape yield Ä or Ü. Those marks are diacritics, not extra alphabetic positions. I and J share a cell; the mechanical output uses I throughout. The initial JA, the later JA, and JEDE are editorial I/J readings.

## Editorial reading and translation

The following is a reading aid, with line continuations joined, word separators removed, HS rendered as modern ss/ß in its four recurring contexts, the apparent D-for-N and B-for-L missing-dot errors corrected, and `u.` expanded. These changes are **not** changes to the literal decoding:

> Ja, verd....! Nicht einmal dir kann ich's mehr recht machen, wie deine letzte, im Übrigen ja, wie auch die andern zwei Künstlerkarten, sehr hübsche und daher von mir mit herzlichem Dank entgegengenommene, in etwas merkwürdigem Tone abgefasste Sonnenkarte. Ich bin froh, dass ich sie selbst vom Postboten entgegennahm. Außerdem muss ich jede Verantwortung für Küdinghovener Filialpostverhältnisse ablehnen. Schluss und Kuss.

English translation, preserving the unfinished construction of the first sentence rather than supplying a missing German verb:

> Yes, damn...! I can no longer even please you, as [with] your latest sunshine postcard-otherwise, like the other two art postcards, very pretty, and therefore received by me with heartfelt thanks, [but] written in a somewhat peculiar tone. I am glad I received it from the postman myself. Furthermore, I must disclaim all responsibility for the branch-post arrangements in Küdinghoven. End, and a kiss.

`VERD....` is an abbreviated exclamation, approximately “damn...”; its full wording is not present. “Sunshine postcard” translates literal SONNENKARTE, without identifying some other lost card. KÜDINGHOVENER is the directly decoded place adjective; geography is supporting context rather than a reason to assign its glyphs. The first sentence is an elliptical comparison/apposition: *wie deine letzte … Sonnenkarte* may presuppose an omitted *z. B.* or comparable connective. This is a linguistic hypothesis, not cipher text. Row 3 ends at `LETZTE, -` and row 4 continues directly with `IM ÜBRIGEN`; no missing cipher line is inferred. The incomplete syntax is retained rather than silently repaired.

- **HS is literal in four places:** AUHSERDEM (259-260), MUHS (268-269), SCHLUHS (341-342), KUHS (346-347). Each first glyph is an undotted cap = H; the following is a dotted top-left corner = S. The period-spelling comparison covers six expected ß words: *außerdem, muß, Schluß, Kuß, daß, abgefaßte*. The first four appear as HS and the last two as SS on the card. This supports the editorial interpretation, but does not establish why the writer chose it or justify replacing the literal H with S. DASS and ABGEFASSTE really do have two dotted top-left corners and remain SS.
- **GEGENGEDOMMENE, position 156:** the ⊐ cell is undotted, hence literal D (`E` in the shape notation). The nearby dotted ⊐ at position 153 is an N control. `work/review1-pos156-D-rawNN.png` and `review1-pos153-N-rawNN.png` retain fresh nearest-neighbor crops from the original. The conventional intended *entgegengenommene* is editorial only. This apparent dropped-dot slip is handled like the B/L case below; no dot is invented.
- **POSTVERHÄBTNISSE, position 321:** the cup is undotted and therefore literally B. The surrounding word supports an intended L in POSTVERHÄLTNISSE, but no missing dot is invented. See `work/l14l.png`.

## Geographic and postal context

The address clearly gives **Kottbuser Damm 72**. The arrival mark reads **Postamte 59, 22/12.99**; 59 agrees with **Berlin S 59** in the address. The Bonn postmark is visible, but its posting day is not safely legible here. The portal's date 21 December 1899 is consistent with arrival on 22 December; it is not a fresh reading of the obscured Bonn date.

Küdinghoven is in Beuel, on the right-bank side of Bonn: the [official Bonn municipal page](https://www.bonn.de/bonn-erleben/besichtigen-entdecken/beuel.php?loc=de) lists it among Beuel's districts and describes Beuel as rechtsrheinisch. This is geographic support for the decoded place adjective alongside the Bonn postmark, not proof of the 1899 administrative status or branch-office details.

The six ß spellings above are the period linguistic comparison raised in Review 1, not six encoded ß glyphs. Modern editorial spelling changes *muß/Schluß/Kuß/daß/abgefaßte* to *muss/Schluss/Kuss/dass/abgefasste*, while *außerdem* retains ß. [Duden's edition history](https://www.duden.de/ueber_duden/auflagengeschichte) supports the 1901/1902 standardization chronology, but a new entry-by-entry historical dictionary verification was not performed. Context and provenance details are also recorded in `work/review1-context.md`; no manuscript intention or historical identity is established by these observations.
