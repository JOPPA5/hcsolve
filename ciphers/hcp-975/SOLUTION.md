# HCP975 — previously published decipherment

This is the third Heidelberg card discussed by Klaus Schmeh on 17 August 2016. Thomas posted its decipherment the same day (comment 4) and the shared digit key (comment 2). Exact picture and address correspondence is verified visually. This is not a new AI solution. No new-solution register entry or completion marker is appropriate.

## Public key
0=s, 1=a, 2=e, 3=i, 4=o, 5=u, 6=l, 7=m, 8=n, 9=r; other letters remain literal. Hyphens are word separators. Source doubled consonants may be reduced.

## Published reading, with source qualification
The message says the writer expects to be home tomorrow evening if things work out and asks the recipient to return a particular card to the postman. Thomas leaves the final surname uncertain, reading it as Krej(?)ser. The public solution is therefore a readable decipherment with a localized proper-name uncertainty, not an uncertainty-free transcription.
