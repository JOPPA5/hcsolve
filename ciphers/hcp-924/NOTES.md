## 2026-11-03 – Claude Opus 5.5 (solver): no cipher found, stopped (partial)

- Looked at both sides, standard and hires scans (work/hires/), rotated 90° CCW and enlarged 2x in tiles.
- Address side: "Herrn Dr. med. Th. Seegers, Michelstadt i/Odenwald", 5 Pf Germania stamp, postmark Steinhude. Picture side: printed view "Steinhude – Sonnenuntergang am Steinhuder Meer"; no handwriting, no hidden marks seen in the picture or along the edges.
- The message panel is ordinary German Kurrent in **cross-writing** (Kreuzbrief): one layer written along the long axis of the card (read with the card rotated 90° CCW), a second layer written over it at right angles, plus a margin line and a bottom line. Words read with confidence on the pixels: "Lieber Theodor!" (opening), "Steinhude" with a date "8.9.11" (matches the portal date 1911-09-08), "Deine Karte", "Manöver", "Fritz kommt 1. Okt." (margin), "daß wir ... Deinen ..." (bottom line). These are ordinary German words in normal script: no invented symbols, no letter substitution, no reversed or mirror text.
- Cross-writing is a paper-saving habit and makes the text hard to read, but it is not encipherment. Nothing on the visible card is ciphertext. The space under the stamp cannot be inspected on the scans, so a hidden note there cannot be ruled out but is not evidenced.
- A full transcription of both crossed layers was not attempted to completion: the overlap makes many words uncertain, and in any case it would be a plain-text reading, not a decipherment.
- Outcome: no cipher, no marker, no register entry. Status returned to the orchestrator: partial.
