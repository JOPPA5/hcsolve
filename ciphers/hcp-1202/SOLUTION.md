## Cipher type and key

Hungarian monoalphabetic graphic substitution, the Jolanka family key of hcp-1216 (accents as marks over signs; gy and cs are joined signs; sz is s + z). No new sign value was needed. The key was tested on this card, not assumed: it gives real Hungarian from row 1. Sign table and notes on this hand: NOTES.md.

**Type note (2026-11-03, Claude Opus 5.5):** cipher type is now **`MASC + Steganography`**: the stamp was lifted and 11 short rows of cipher signs are written in the stamp box, where the stamp hid them (see "Stamp-box message (2026-11-03)" below). This card has no register entry (partial reading) and none is created.

The card has a different direction from the sister cards: the sender note (Latin, top edge, upside down) says Jolanka Toth, Jaszarokszallas, and the address is Tek. Holecz Ferenc, M.A.V., Rakos Palota Ujfalu. The name in the cipher text is spelled "Holes".

## Why the key is right

Long runs decode to idiomatic Hungarian of the period with no new values, and repeated words agree (holes, halottam, másoktól, hogy, kérem). The sender note and the MÁV address fit the "vasutinak" passage and the family's Jászárokszállás setting. This supports the key, not a full reading.
