## Method and key

Monoalphabetic substitution with digits and letter-shaped signs. One sign stands for the Czech digraph CH. `H` is the transcription label for the handwritten CH sign, which looks like an r with a caron. `y` labels the angular 7-shaped J sign. The table is a literal base-letter substitution; Czech diacritics are not encoded.

| Sign | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 8 | b | c | d | f | g | h | m | n | p | q | r | s | t | y | H |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plain | Y | U | O | I | E | A | L | P | S | T | V | K | R | N | M | B | Z | H | C | D | J | CH |

The key is identical to the family key in ciphers/hcp-1545/work/key.json. Only the labels for J and CH differ.

The transcription was made manually from the source image, **not by OCR**. It is generated by work/build_record.py into work/transcription.json; the key is in work/key.json. The nine main rows (B01–B09) are read from the picture rotated 90 degrees clockwise. The two margin rows (M01–M02) are read from the picture rotated 180 degrees. work/panel-*.png show each row crop with an exact position legend. ## Editorial Czech with diacritics

Diacritics and capitals are editorial. ODOJETI and KOMISSI keep their decoded spellings.

Tisícerý dík, dušinko moje, za drahé psánko, které mne velice potěšilo. Také já vzpomínám vřele na loňského svatého Václava. Musil jsem odložiti svoji cestu do Ústí následkem toho, že náš vrchní správce musil včera odojeti na úřední komissi do Chocně. Pojedu nyní hned odsud z úřadu, kde mám již svá zavazadla. Budu mnoho vzpomínati na svou zlatou Anušku, kterou vřele zdravím a tisíckráte líbám.

Odesílám lístek již z Prahy, poněvadž poštovní cesta z Ústí do Žirovnice trvá asi dva dny.

## English translation

A thousand thanks, my darling, for your dear little letter, which pleased me very much. I too remember last year's St Wenceslas' Day warmly. I had to postpone my journey to Ústí, because yesterday our chief administrator had to leave for an official commission in Choceň. I shall go now, straight from the office, where I already have my luggage. I shall think a great deal of my golden Anuška, whom I greet warmly and kiss a thousand times.

I am sending the card from Prague already, because the post from Ústí to Žirovnice takes about two days.

Musel som odložiť svoju cestu do Ústí, pretože náš hlavný správca musel včera odcestovať na úradnú komisiu do Chocne. Pôjdem teraz hneď odtiaľto z úradu, kde už mám svoju batožinu. ## Address side and remaining marks

The unencrypted address reads: `Velectěná Slečna / slečna Anna Tichá / učitelka v / Žirovnici / u Počátek.` There is no cipher text on this side. The Austrian five-heller stamp is cancelled **PRAHA 7**. On the left is an inverted arrival postmark ŽIROVNICE / SEROWITZ. Its day 27 and year 03 are clear; the month is faint, and the review read it as IX. The card was therefore posted in Prague and, if the month is IX, reached Žirovnice one day after the written date 26 Sep. This independently confirms "Odesílám lístek již z Prahy" in M01. The bilingual printed headings and the collection watermark are not cipher text. The picture side carries the publisher's line `Meissner & Buch, Leipzig. 12 Künstler-Postkarten Serie 1212. Aus dem Obstgarten. gesetzl. geschützt.` It is printed matter. The card has no signature.

## Uncertainty and supporting checks

- **U01, B01 `y5,` (MOJE,).** The printed red cherry covers the tops of these signs. Below it the following are visible: the angular 7-shaped descender of y/J, which matches `yc5n` JSEM (B03) and `3t3y5d4` (B05) and not the hooked q/Z; the lower bowl of a 5/E; then a long slanting stroke. The baseline is visible across the whole span, and nothing else fits between these marks. The reading JE is therefore based on the pixels; the vocative "dušinko moje" agrees with it. The last stroke is longer than this writer's other commas, so it is only a **probable comma**. See work/claude/moje.png and review/work/u01*_x*.png.
- **U02, M01 R of PRAHY (`bh6r1`).** The sign is not visible: it is either under the printed leaf stem or extremely faint. It is **restored** from the context and from the PRAHA 7 postmark.
- B03 last word: the sign is q/Z, not y/J. It has a loop and a descending hook; compare q5/ZE (B04) and angular y4q/JIZ (B07) in work/q-vs-y-same-card.png. Reading: ODLOZITI.
- ODOJETI (B05 `3t3y5d4`) is really written that way; the review confirmed this at 8×. The writer's spelling is kept, and the standard form would be odjeti. KOMISSI with -ss- is a normal spelling around 1900.
- Ústí is most likely Ústí nad Orlicí, near Choceň, but the card does not say so. The sender is not named on this card; on sister cards he signs "Pepouš".
- The scan is only 1024×674 px, which limits fine stroke detail.

All eleven cipher rows are in the record. The transferred key covers every sign and gives continuous, idiomatic Czech of the period. Its content agrees with the date: St Wenceslas' Day is 28 Sep, two days after 26 Sep. It also agrees with the address (Žirovnice) and with both postmarks.
