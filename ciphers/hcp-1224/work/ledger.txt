# HCP1224 sign ledger, Claude Opus 5.5, 2026-11-02. Read by eye from enlarged crops of the hires address side; one token = one written sign.
# Tokens: a letter = the family sign of that letter. O: U: = sign with dots/marks above (ö/ő, ü/ű). GY, CS, SZ-free: sz and zs are written S Z / Z S.
# D = joined long+short stroke (d; 4-like in rows 1, 5, 8, 16). H:h H:p H:d = tall stroke + short companion; the value after the colon rests on word sense (h/p/d near-homographs).
# plain:xxx = written in clear. X = struck-through two-stroke sign (row 21). Q = the undotted curved sign of row 26 (o or undotted i; not z), decoded {o|i?}. Every other doubtful call is listed in SOLUTION.md.
# Acute accents are often faint and are not transcribed.
