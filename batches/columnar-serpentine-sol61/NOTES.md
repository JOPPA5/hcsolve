The new model alternates up/down **by ciphertext readout rank**, testing either starting direction. It does not optimize arbitrary column reversal flags. Equal-length chunks or each one-long hypothesized chunking are reversed at the appropriate alternating ranks, then passed to the unchanged independent frozen 10,000-wide fragment beam. The only copied C++ modification is chunk normalization. Other irregular remainder patterns are not implemented. Historical use of this direction rule is unproved.
