# Sources and evidence boundaries

- Read-only reusable frozen scoring/search implementation: `batches/ring-profile-sol61/ring_profile.cpp`, `ring_profile_masc.cpp`, `run.py`, `refine5.py`, `REPORT.md`; joint refinement from `batches/kryha-framing-sol61/ring_ic_refine.cpp` and `ring_ic_refine5.cpp`. This batch changes the model to one shared pair of rings fitted to two separately reset messages, with n-grams never spanning their boundary.
- Old original 17-step schedule and direction/ring conventions were reconstructed in `batches/kryha-method-sol61/` and `batches/kryha-wheel-sol61/` from primary Callimahos, *Military Cryptanalytics*, Part III, printed pp. 184–201. Archival document: https://www.governmentattic.org/41docs/NSAmilitaryCryptalyticsPt3_1977.pdf . This is a restricted earlier hardware model, not proof of the Slovak sender's keying or stepping in 1944.
- SSA NR2417 (1942), https://cryptocellar.org/kryha/nr2417.pdf , documents newer programmable stops and selective extra advancement after plaintext J in some German commercial traffic. Neither later programmable settings nor content-dependent stepping are searched here. No historical attribution to Slovak traffic follows from this pilot.
- Frozen models: `ciphers/hcp-350/work/ring-unigrams.tsv`, `ring-4grams.tsv`, `ring-5grams.tsv`. Independent held-out texts: `ciphers/hcp-393/work/sk_snk-ud-train.conllu` after the first-80% training boundary. Source attribution is in the originating corpus/model work directories. Their first-80% training material is never used to construct these controls.
- Literal working bodies: `ciphers/hcp-350/work/transcription.txt` and `ciphers/hcp-351/work/transcription.txt`, each 385 letters. Full input bytes and hashes are recorded in `input-provenance.json`.
- Source-supported alternatives: `batches/source-variants-350-351/manifest.json` and its two preferred-body files. The independent source audit, `batches/framing-source-audit-sol61/REPORT.md`, distinguishes scan readings from uncertain chronology, administrative indicators and shared-key hypotheses. Only the complete supplied preferred pair is tested as an alternate; no selective or extra correction is introduced.

No published solution, target plaintext, new source download, hidden API, watcher or reviewer was used. Original cipher directories and source metadata are read-only. No marker is written.
