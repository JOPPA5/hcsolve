# Rare input-label ambiguity in the near-complete two-mask text

Input is the unchanged blind candidate `batches/kryha-two-mask-refine-sol61/actual-row5-pure6-r7-full.json` (first row). Raw letters are never silently normalized. This is forensic analysis, not a claim that the whole cipher is solved.

The fixed physical key was tested with all325 input-ring swaps plus identity under frozen TRAIN6/7. Pure6 retains the unchanged P; pure7 prefers Q→J (`telejrafui`). Thus neither objective alone settles rare labels. A second explicitly restricted label diagnostic enumerates all120 permutations of I/J/G/Q/X, followed by the same325 swaps+identity (39,120 candidates). Pure6 favors X→J sentence delimiters and G→X (`existuielacneisia`), leaving `teleqrafui`. Pure7 has tied rare-label alternatives, including Q→G with G→Q (`eqistuie`, `telegrafui`). No complete coherent unique input-alphabet choice emerges. Full raw retained keys and whole355-letter pairs are saved, separately labelled by method and score. All400 retained pairs independently reencrypt every literal source-strong body symbol through adjacent physical wheel distances; this proves mechanical consistency only.

`locations.json` records every I/J/G/Q/X position with context. The candidate has no J. Several linguistically plausible J locations render I (`nailacneisia`, `predavaiuci`, `existuie`, `tele...fui`); genuine I occupies the same input slot (e.g. `kupit`, `tisic`, `tridni`). A bijective global I↔J swap cannot repair both. These putative J errors span many physical residues, so a single wrong ordinal is insufficient to explain all of them. An I/J-preparation merger is an inference to investigate, not an independently documented Slovak rule.

The lone G at body index207, residue0, appears in `egistuie`; the lone Q at338, residue16, appears in `teleqrafui`. Those residues differ from the sole A/B mask difference (ordinal4). They are not automatically explained by that differing stop. A multi-label cycle may trade a sentence separator for G/X spelling, but selecting it by expected words would force plaintext. No symbol has been repaired in the sources.

A primary historical comparator is SSA memorandum NR2417, Levine/Lewis August1942, page1: https://cryptocellar.org/kryha/nr2417.pdf . It describes reserving J as a word separator and making an extra lever press after it in the German commercial system. It does not establish Slovak I/J merging, this traffic's separator, or the same operating procedure. The current successful model assumes one ordinary press per literal letter; adopting that extra press would require a separately verified mechanical model. The observed candidate separator-like letters occur only at sentence boundaries, not every inferred word boundary.

Root's independent full385 extension finds substantial text in the previously omitted20-prefix/10-suffix as well. Therefore those30 letters must no longer be treated as a proven header/trailer.
