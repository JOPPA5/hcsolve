# 364 raw EXCUU: a single V→U error elsewhere

This batch tests a distinct conditional source-error hypothesis. It retains the corrected literal EXCUU reading, omits original group 3 ITPKA, and hypothesizes that one expected ciphertext V was transmitted or typed as U at a position outside the two ambiguous final U slots of original group 12. It does not change the source or assert that such an error occurred.

The retained 65-letter raw body has four U positions. Excluding its group-12 slots 54/55 leaves body positions 37/38 (1 based), corresponding to the two Us in original group 9 TUUTE. Replacing either one by V yields exactly two finite ciphertext variants. Both inventories equal the quoted four-sentence 64-letter X template plus one additional X. This test therefore covers TUVTE versus TVUTE at that group, while leaving literal EXCUU untouched. Earlier tests changing EXCUU itself to EXCUV/EXCVU are outside this branch.

The faithful exact-cover columnar solver searches three width-16 transpositions with arbitrary independent permutation keys, the quote template and a free fifth-X insertion position. The two ciphertext alternatives form an explicit finite union. Sixteen common alignment cases n=1..16 and fourteen progressive cases (n,n+1,n+2), n=1..14, are each bounded to 15,000 ms. No true key, chosen error position, or planted filler position is provided to the solver.

Before actual cases, three fresh 65-letter quote controls were generated with random independent keys: common alignments 8 and 16, and progressive alignments 8/9/10. Each injects V→U at a recorded ciphertext position, then searches the complete set of observed-U→V variants. All three recover the exact full planted plaintext and expected ciphertext; key identity is not required. The solver receives only the template, observed-derived union, and alignments. Independent full-length encoders and inverse decoders verify the recovered keys. Inverse-decoding literal corrupted ciphertext and re-encoding it reproduces that literal ciphertext exactly, with a single U/V plaintext mismatch from the recovered quote. Controls take approximately 1.07,1.26,1.16 seconds. Full truth/input/result artifacts are retained.

All **30 actual cases are UNSAT**, with zero SAT and zero UNKNOWN. This excludes only the declared quote, omission, two error-position alternatives, width-16 common/progressive alignment families and free-fifth-X model. Actual result counts are in `summary.json`, with every case and timeout retained in its `target-*.json` and the aggregate `targets.json`. A SAT would be a conditional one-symbol-error fit, not a glyph correction or independently established plaintext; a timeout remains UNKNOWN. No register entry, source edit, marker, or completed-solution claim is produced here.

Run from repository root: `python3 batches/r364-raw-U-error-sol61/run.py`. Saved per-case results are reused, and incomplete scans resume only missing cases. `input.json` records the exact raw body, exclusion positions, two variants, quote template, and source hash.
