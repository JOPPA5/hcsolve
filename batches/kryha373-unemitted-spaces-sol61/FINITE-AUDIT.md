# Exact finite helper audit

`python3 batches/kryha373-unemitted-spaces-sol61/audit-finite.py` checks 20 independently constructed affine cases against direct integer enumeration of all 676 mod-26 pairs, without using the CRT expressions in the reference evaluator. Ten satisfiable and ten deliberately impossible partial-injection cases agree. Cases include zero and even coefficients, independent free variables on unused ring slots, and completion of each accepted partial ring to a full permutation.

For each field, Gaussian elimination expresses the linear solution space as affine functions of its free coordinates. The observed ring expressions depend only on the free coordinates returned by their variable support. Enumerating those coordinates therefore enumerates every possible observed-ring assignment; setting inactive coordinates to zero selects an extension of that assignment. For affine expressions over GF(2) and GF(13), the base evaluation and unit-coordinate differences recover their exact coefficients. CRT combines residues by `a13 + 13*((a2-a13) mod 2)`, which uniquely covers positions 0..25.

A partial ring assignment extends to a permutation exactly when its observed values are distinct. The known plaintext/ciphertext rows never contain an unobserved letter slot. Both A slots used for gauge fixing are included in the observed set. Filling absent letters with unused positions thus changes no equation or gauge condition. This argument applies to the ordinary unblocked solve. Blocked uniqueness queries intentionally use SMT, not this enumeration.

The helper limit of 50,000 is a dispatch threshold, not a truncated search: larger products use the existing SMT solver with its declared timeout and preserve UNKNOWN. Variable support may contain algebraically cancelled variables; retaining those only increases work and cannot remove an assignment. The helper expects the affine modular expressions produced by `crt.expressions`; it is not a general nonlinear solver.

Saved positive controls are retained: eight independently encoded physical-mask prefixes of 92/93 emitted letters, all 51 periods 2..52, each uniquely accepting its planted 20/23/30 period. Held-out 20-letter checks recover 20,18,20,20,20,19,20,20 letters respectively. Imperfect held-out fits remain disclosed; full-prefix fitting is not physical-wheel/key identification.
